It wasn’t intended to be functional the class was more focused on getting the student used to the mobile app creator and setting up the framework. It was made earlier this year and was very barebones. I included it into my ePortfolio because creating mobile apps is a big part of the tech world and showing competence in this field helps me to stand out. Furthermore, it includes programing languages that I haven’t used before like XML and is unique in what I have worked with in the past. It was improved by giving it SQL databases instead of just storing the data in the program. Also, the display for the data was made cleaner and consistent.
I planned for 2 and 3, but I believe this enhancement got 5 and 4. All outcomes have been achieved but which enhancement goes to which has been changed. I learned about fragments and realized that they aren’t something that I can replace activities with. I intend to use them in other projects, but this isn’t really suited for it.
